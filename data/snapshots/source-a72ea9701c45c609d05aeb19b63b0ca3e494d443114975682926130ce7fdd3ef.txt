Getting Started as a 
VPAM
 System Administrator



                                                        
This guide helps you configure a newly deployed 
VPAM
 server for its first production use.



                                                        
Starting with your System Administrator credentials, you'll configure the platform, create your first protected resources, and validate the deployment using a test internal user.



                                                        
When you finish this guide, your PAS environment will be ready to onboard production users.



                                                        
Who Should Use This Guide



                                                        
This guide is intended for:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
New 
VPAM
 System Administrators.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Administrators taking ownership of an existing 
VPAM
 deployment.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Technical staff learning the recommended 
VPAM
deployment workflow.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
To follow his guide, no previous 
VPAM
 experience is required.



                                                        
Before You Begin



                                                        
Before starting this guide, ensure that:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
Your 
VPAM
 server has been deployed.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
You have System Administrator credentials.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
You meet network and infrastructure 
Requirements
.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
What You'll Accomplish



                                                        
This guide is organized into implementation phases. Each phase builds on the previous one and ends with a validation step. In this guide, you will:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
Access your 
VPAM
 server.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Secure the platform.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Design your access model.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Configure protected resources.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Configure third-party access.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Create a test internal user.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Validate the first privileged session.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
What This Guide Does Not Cover



                                                        
This guide focuses on the initial platform configuration. The following topics are covered in separate documentation:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
High Availability (HA)



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Disaster Recovery (DR)



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Nexus deployments



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
REST API and SDK



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Syslog and SIEM integrations



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
HTTPS Credential Passthrough



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Advanced authentication architectures



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Zero Trust guidance



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Advanced auditing and reporting



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
 



                                                        
1. Access your 
VPAM
 Server



                                                        
Verify you have access to the 
VPAM
 User Interface before continuing with this guide.



                                                        
Read 
Access and Login
 to complete your first sign in.



                                                        
Read the 
User Interface Reference
 to understand different sections of the 
VPAM
 interface.



                                                        
This first login confirms that your deployment is operational and that your administrator account has the permissions required to complete the remaining phases of this guide.



                                                        
After you validate you have System Administrator's access to the UI, continue to configure your server.



                                                        
2. Secure the Platform



                                                        
In this step, you will configure the authentication and security settings that protect access to your PAS environment. Completing this step determines who can access the platform, how they authenticate, and what safeguards protect privileged access.



                                                        
2.1 Review Your Authentication Strategy

                                                            

                                                                
Determine how your Internal Users will authenticate to the server. Depending on your deployment, you may choose one of the following authentication methods:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Local authentication



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
LDAP



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
SAML Single Sign-On (SSO)



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
Select the authentication method that aligns with your organization's identity management strategy before creating users.



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
2.2 Configure Authentication

                                                            

                                                                
Complete the authentication configuration in the Legacy UI only, using the following guides:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
SAML Entra ID Configuration

                                                                        



                                                                        
Use this guide to configure SAML for SSO in your server using Entra ID.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Other Identity Providers

                                                                        



                                                                        
Use this guide to configure SAML for SSO in your server using other Identity Providers.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Authentication Strategy Recommendations

                                                                        



                                                                        
Use this guide to follow recommended practices for your server.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
To complete the SAML configuration for Entra ID or other IdPs, confirm the following:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
You have administrative access to the IdP.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
You have the required certificates and metadata to configure the identity exchange.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
You have the required user attributes correctly mapped.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
NOTE: 

                                                                    
When configuring SAML authentication, remember that enabling SSO changes the server's authentication method immediately. Perform this change during a planned maintenance window to minimize user disruption.



                                                                



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
2.3 Configure Multi-Factor Authentication

                                                            

                                                                
Request that Internal Users and 
vendors
  use a second factor authentication to log in to the server with the following guide:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Multi-Factor Authentication in Legacy UI

                                                                        



                                                                        
Use this guide to configure MFA when SAML with SSO is enabled. This regulates access to the server.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
2.4 Configure Passwords and Accounts Settings

                                                            

                                                                
Set password policies and account rules for disablement.



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
In the New UI, navigate to 
System Administration
 menu > 
Authentication Settings
 sub-menu > 
Passwords & Accounts
 tab.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
In the Legacy UI, navigate to 
System Admin
 top menu > 
Settings
 drop-down >  
Passwords & Accounts
.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
2.5 Configure Authentication Requirements

                                                            

                                                                
Use the following guide to configure access to 
applications
 at a server and 
vendor
-level.



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Authentication Settings in the New UI

                                                                        



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
2.6 Create a Locally Authenticated Emergency Account

                                                            

                                                                
A Locally Authenticated System Admin Emergency Account remains independent of external authentication provider. This type of account enables you to have emergency access to your server in case of lockout or when normal authentication is unavailable; for example:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
During an Identity Provider outage



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
During a disaster 



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
As a result of an authentication configuration issue



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
To create a Locally Authenticated System Admin Emergency Account:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Navigate to 
User Management
 > 
Users
 > 
Add New User
.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Check 
User is an Administrator with full system access
.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Complete the form.



                                                                        
IMPORTANT: 

                                                                            
You must have a second email account with an approved domain.



                                                                        



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Save the user.



                                                                        
The email receives a message with instructions to create a local password.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Safekeep the user ID and password for emergency use.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
IMPORTANT: 

                                                                    
System Administrators are the sole accountable owners of the protection and safekeeping of the Locally Authenticated System Admin Emergency Accounts. System Administrators must store its user ID and password following their own security protocols.



                                                                



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
2.7 Configure Authorized Domains

                                                            

                                                                
Navigate to 
System Administration
 menu > 
System Settings
 sub-menu > 
Authorized Domains
 section to review and configure the list of Authorized Domains.



                                                                
Authorized Domains define the email domains that can be used when creating users and become the default domain presented during user creation.



                                                                
Verify the following:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Your organization's primary domain is configured.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Unused domains are removed, if appropriate.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
The correct Primary Domain has been selected.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
NOTE: 

                                                                    
The system requires at least one Authorized Domain at all times.



                                                                



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
3. Design Your Access Model



                                                        
In this step, you separate permissions, internal user membership, and organizational ownership into different objects. Designing these objects before onboarding users creates a scalable access model that is easier to manage, audit, and maintain as your deployment grows.



                                                        
In this step, you do not create any users yet. Instead, you create the logical categories where each user fits.



                                                        
Understanding the Access Model



                                                        

                                                            
VPAM

                                                            
 uses three primary objects to manage user access:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
User Groups
: Determine what a user can access.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Roles
: Determine what a user can do.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Departments
: Determines the team that a user belongs to.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
Although these objects work together, each serves a different purpose.



                                                        
To design your Access Model, read the recommendations:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
Create User Groups
: Provide granular and specific access to 
applications
 and other assets in your server.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Define the Roles
: Define them based on what your team members are required to do. Ensure to limit permissions to follow a zero-trust access model.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Build your Departments Structure
: Base your departments on your company's structure to facilitate visibility and accountability across your organization.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        

                                                            
4.
 Configure Protected Resources



                                                        
Protected Resources
 are the systems, applications, services, and credentials that internal users
 and vendors
 access through 
VPAM
.



                                                        
Before onboarding internal users or granting third-party access, configure the protected resources that will be managed by the platform. This ensures that access can be assigned and validated during the remaining phases of this guide.



                                                        
4.
1 Connectivity Components

                                                            

                                                                
Configure the following assets:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Gatekeeper Installation

                                                                        



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Gateway Configuration

                                                                        



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Applications

                                                                        



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
Verify that the required connectivity components have been deployed, registered, and are communicating with the server before continuing.



                                                                
TIP: 

                                                                    


Remember the difference between a Gatekeeper and a Gateway:


A Gateway functions as a secure entry point for support agents to access remotely to applications that do not require built-in services. 


On the other hand, a Gatekeeper provides built-in services to support agents that access a single application in your systems.



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
4.
2 Services

                                                            

                                                                
Configure the 
Services
 that define how users connect to each protected resource.



                                                                
Depending on your deployment, services may include remote access protocols, file transfer capabilities, or other connection methods supported by the server. 



                                                                
NOTE: 

                                                                    
Only 
Gatekeepers
 provide built-in services such as Desktop Sharing and File Transfer, and you can configure additional services as needed.



                                                                



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
4.
3 Configure Secrets and Associate Them to Resources

                                                            

                                                                
Create, store, and assign secrets required to authenticate to your protected resources.



                                                                
To complete this process:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Create 
Secrets in the New UI
 or 
Credentials in the Legacy UI
.



                                                                        
NOTE: 

                                                                            
The new UI provides more functionality to secrets than the legacy UI does to credentials.



                                                                        



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Assign them to 
Services
.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                                
By assigning credentials to a service, when an Internal User or Customer attempts to initiate a connection to a protected service, the 
VPAM
 server can inject the credential without the user or customer ever looking at the credential.



                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
4.
4 Test the Connections

                                                            

                                                                
Ensure that you have access to the 
applications
 and that each one is communicating successfully with the server. Look for the following signs of success:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
The target system's Gatekeepers on 
http://localhost:4680/
 display green 
Registered
 status.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
The 
Applications
 list contain all your configured resources and they have a 
Ready
 status.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
You, as a System Administrator, can connect to 
an application
 after downloading and installing the 
Connection Manager
.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
You can select a secret to 
Launch
 a protected service.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
You can disconnect from a session and end the connection without issues.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                            



                                                        



                                                        
5. Configure Vendor Access



                                                        
In this step, you configure the organizational objects required to provide secure vendor access to protected resources. It is recommended that you already have Applications before you configure your vendors.



                                                        
To complete this step:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
Create a 
Vendor
.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Select the Applications that its Vendor Reps have access to.



                                                                
Ensure you configure:



                                                                

                                                                    

                                                                        
Approvals per Application
 and 
Approval Profiles
: Enforce internal users and vendor reps to request access before connecting to an application.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Authorized Domains
: Prevent people from outside your vendor's network to sign in to your server.



                                                                    



                                                                    

                                                                        
Authentication Requirements
: Configure at 
server-level
 to create a global policy and configure at 
vendor-level
 to override global policy.



                                                                    



                                                                

                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Test the connection.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
Repeat the process with all your vendors.



                                                        
Read 
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Validation for Nexus Connections



                                                        
6. Create a Test Internal User and Validate Your Deployment



                                                        
In this step, you create and configure a test internal user to validate your deployment. A test user allows you to confirm that authentication, authorization, protected resources, and permissions work together as expected. Identifying configuration issues now helps prevent onboarding problems later.



                                                        
TIP: 

                                                            
Use a non-production account for this validation.



                                                        



                                                        
Follow the steps to 
Add a New Internal User
.



                                                        
Implement each Authentication Strategy and Access Model for this user.



                                                        
Validate what the user can do, 
depending on its role
. In a successful deployment, internal users can:



                                                        

                                                            

                                                                
View, create, edit, and delete 
Applications
.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
View, create, edit, and delete 
Vendors
.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Initiate connections with the 
Connection Manager
.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Use secrets to launch privileged sessions.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Manage secrets in the server.



                                                            



                                                            

                                                                
Approve or reject connection requests.



                                                            



                                                        

                                                        
IMPORTANT: 

                                                            
Ensure that, depending on the User Group and Department, the user can only interact with resources assigned to that User Group and Department.



                                                        



                                                        
NOTE: 

                                                            
This process takes a considerable amount of time, but it enables you to test and confirm your deployment is ready for production. Be patient!
